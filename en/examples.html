Use cases

Seven real-world scenarios where an AI agent’s access must not amount to permission to act.

The principle

An AI agent may have access to a system without having the authority to carry out every action. Adaptive Runtime provides an independent control layer. It checks the conditions for each action, allows it when authorised, requires human approval where necessary, or blocks it. It also records evidence of the decision, without replacing existing systems.
