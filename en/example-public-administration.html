PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION

An application decided without proper authority

Scenario

An agent supports the processing of an application, from reviewing the submission to preparing the notification.

Without control

Instructions disguised within an application could influence the agent to approve and notify the applicant without a decision by the authorised official.

With Adaptive Runtime

  • Reviewing the application and preparing a proposed decision can be automated.
  • The decision remains with the authorised official; content supplied by the applicant must not be treated as instructions to the agent.
  • The evidence record captures the action taken, its basis and the human decision.

Rules and thresholds are tailored to the organisation’s systems and policies during the assessment.
