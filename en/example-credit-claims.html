BANKING AND INSURANCE

A credit or insurance decision without accountability

Scenario

An agent reviews credit applications or insurance claims, including supporting documents, and prepares a proposed decision.

Without control

The agent could approve or reject an application without oversight. A malicious attachment might influence its decision, with no clear human accountability.

With Adaptive Runtime

  • Document review and the preparation of a recommendation can be automated.
  • The final decision requires human involvement, with visibility of the amount involved and the impact on the customer.
  • Evidence is retained of who made the decision and which rules were applied.

Rules and thresholds are tailored to the organisation’s systems and policies during the assessment.
