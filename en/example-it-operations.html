IT OPERATIONS

Dangerous changes to live systems

Scenario

An agent investigates an incident, reviews logs, prepares a fix in GitHub and interacts with Kubernetes.

Without control

With overly broad credentials, the agent could merge unreviewed changes, grant itself administrator privileges or disrupt critical services.

With Adaptive Runtime

  • Reading information and proposing changes can proceed without being blocked.
  • Changes to live systems or the granting of additional privileges require approval; irreversible actions may be rejected.
  • During an incident, the agent’s access can be suspended using the configured capabilities and mechanisms, with its actions recorded.

Rules and thresholds are tailored to the organisation’s systems and policies during the assessment.
