CUSTOMER SERVICE AND CLAIMS

A fraudulent refund or compromised account

Scenario

An agent handles customer requests by email and chat through the CRM, including refunds and claims settlements.

Without control

A carefully crafted message could prompt the agent to approve an improper refund or change the email address associated with an account, allowing a third party to take it over.

With Adaptive Runtime

  • Routine responses and requests can proceed without intervention.
  • Refunds above defined limits, sensitive compensation payments and changes to account access details require human approval.
  • Instructions embedded in untrusted content should be filtered by the configured controls; every decision is documented.

Rules and thresholds are tailored to the organisation’s systems and policies during the assessment.
