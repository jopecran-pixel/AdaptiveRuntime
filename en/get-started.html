Get started

Three steps, each ending in a decision. None commits you to the next.

  1. A 30-minute conversation

    Which agents you have or are planning, which systems they reach, and where one of the examples applies. If it makes sense, we show the demonstration.

  2. An assessment

    A review of the agents, the tools they reach and the actions that commit the organisation. The result is an exposure map and the proposed rules.

  3. A four-week pilot

    In one system and one team. In the first week the rules run in observation, showing what they would have stopped; from the second week they are enforced. We measure what went through on its own, what waited for a person and what was refused.
